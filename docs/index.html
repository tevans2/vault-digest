Vault Digest

Vault Digest is a personal plugin for Obsidian that shows tasks, deadlines and notes on one dashboard. It is a personal project used by its author and is not offered as a public service.

What it does with Google Calendar

With your permission, it creates, updates and deletes calendar events for tasks you choose to put on your calendar, and keeps those events aligned with your Obsidian notes. It also reads your calendar list so you can choose which calendar to use.

Everything runs on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the author.
